# Privacy Policy for OwnedIt

_Last updated: March 2026_

## Overview

OwnedIt is designed to keep your data private and on your device. The app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage

All inventory data — including items, rooms, photos, receipts, and reminders — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device except as described below.

## iCloud Sync

When iCloud sync is enabled, your data is synced to your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) and is not accessible to the developers of OwnedIt.

## Barcode Lookup

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to [UPCitemdb](https://www.upcitemdb.com) to look up product information such as name and brand. No personal information is transmitted as part of this request. You can review UPCitemdb's privacy policy at upcitemdb.com.

## Photos and Documents

Photos and documents you attach to items are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to any server operated by OwnedIt.

## Notifications

Maintenance reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. No data is transmitted to any server to deliver these notifications.

## Analytics and Advertising

OwnedIt does not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporting services, or advertising SDKs. No usage data is collected or transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

OwnedIt does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [OwnedIt GitHub repository](https://github.com/azheng888/OwnedIt).
